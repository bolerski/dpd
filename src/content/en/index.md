The „Društvo pod dudom“ association from Morović is a non-governmental, non-profit organization founded to protect, preserve and improve the environment, to raise ecological awareness and to encourage active citizen participation in preserving nature.
Through volunteer actions, educational programs, and sporting and cultural events, we work to create a healthier and more responsible environment for all generations.
