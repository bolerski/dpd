A year has passed since our first clean-up action. The association was only just being formed. Some of us met for the first time at that very first gathering, others joined later.
At first glance, a group of completely different types of people, yet we do have something in common – a love of nature and a healthy environment. 🌳🌲🌿
We returned to where, in a way, it all began.
Another successfully completed action is behind us. We collected the waste that had piled up since our last action a year ago at this location, as well as waste that had been there far longer, because this time we ventured deeper into the forest. 👣🌳🍁🍂
What is important to mention is that the circle of volunteers is growing, and among them are more and more children who are becoming aware of the importance of preserving clean nature and the environment. 😊
Let us set a good example for children and not leave waste behind us. Let us protect what we have, because there is no replacement for it. 🌍🍀💚
