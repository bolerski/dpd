Welcoming spring, on Saturday 28 February 2026 Društvo pod dudom continued its outdoor activities aimed at preserving our environment. 💚
The Morović clean-up brought together members of the association, friends, enthusiasts and – something that especially delights us and gives us hope that the need for such actions will decrease in the future – a large number of children. 🫂
Nice weather, good will and hardworking hands helped us send important messages about our relationship with nature, homeland and togetherness, and a well-earned reward followed the job well done – homemade wood-fired pizza and good company. 🍕
