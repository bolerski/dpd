date: 2025-12-04

🌳 The association „Društvo pod dudom“, in cooperation with the „Branko Radičević“ Primary School from Šid, is launching an initiative that we hope will grow into a tradition – „The Root of Our Generation“ – dedicated to nurturing ecological awareness and togetherness among students. 👫🏼👫🏻
🍃 As part of this initiative, first-grade students planted young saplings of Japanese cherry, Judas tree, tulip tree, ornamental hawthorn and linden in the schoolyard, taking the first step toward creating their future green space.
🌱 Our idea and goal is for each successive generation to receive its own tree, which they will care for throughout their schooling and watch grow together with them. In this way students develop responsibility, respect for nature and a sense of belonging to the school and community.
💌 The initiative aims to create lasting values, but also to remind us all that great processes begin with small yet meaningful steps.
